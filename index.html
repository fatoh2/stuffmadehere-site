About Shane

Shane Wighton is an engineer who used to lead an engineering team at Formlabs, the 3D‑printer company, and now builds things in his own shop for a camera. He has a degree in mechanical engineering, a master’s in computer science, five patents and 17,342 pounds of tools. The channel started in March 2020 with a video about bending steel with plastic. Since then the machines have aimed bows, swung bats, cut hair, carved pumpkins, picked locks, peeled apples and, more than anything, refused to let anyone miss a shot. His wife tests most of them and is not easily impressed. This site is made by a fan and is not his; every title and picture here belongs to him. Watch the channel.
